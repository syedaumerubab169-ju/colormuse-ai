Privacy

The photo is read inside this page and drawn on your screen. It is never sent anywhere. Clearing the photo or closing the page removes it. Saved palettes keep only colors and names, never the photo.

ColorMuse AI is a styling aid and not a medical or dermatological tool. Results are estimates and depend on lighting and your camera.

Drop a photo here or choose one

Taking a selfie opens your phone's camera app.

Tap a cheek or the forehead in even light. Or analyze as is and the page will measure inside the dashed oval.

For the most reliable reading

  • Stand by a window in daylight, facing the light.
  • Turn off beauty filters, flash and color effects.
  • Remove heavy makeup if you can, and avoid strong colored walls.
  • Tap a cheek or forehead, away from shadows and shiny spots.

No photo handy?

Try a demo skin tone to see what the results look like. These are sample colors, not a measurement of you.

Camera not opening?

Selfie opens your phone's camera app, so this page never asks for camera access itself. If nothing opens, go to Android Settings, Apps, your browser, Permissions, and allow Camera. You can also take the photo in your camera app and use Upload a photo.
